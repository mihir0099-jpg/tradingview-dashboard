1. The Core Idea: Markets Move in Circles (Octaves)

Just like a clock face has 360 degrees in one complete rotation, stock price movements expand and contract in mathematical harmonic angles discovered by legendary trader W.D. Gann.

When a stock starts a swing from any major pivot (Anchor High or Anchor Low), it does not move randomly. It rotates through exact geometric checkpoints:

  • 90° (Quarter Cycle): First pause / Scalp profit target.
  • 180° (Half Cycle / The Trampoline): The most critical balance line. If breached, 91.1% historical probability of racing to 360°!
  • 270° (Three-Quarter Extension): High-speed continuation runway.
  • 360° (Full Octave Wall): Complete 1-cycle rotation. Major institutional profit booking.
  • 720° (Double Octave Ceiling): Extreme exhaustion. 81.8% of big rallies stop here, with a ~40% sharp reversal risk.

5. The Gann Mathematical Formula Behind the Radar

You can calculate these exact levels by hand or in Excel using Gann's Square of 9 formula:

UPSIDE TARGETS (From Swing Low):
Target Level = ( √Low + θ / 180 )²

DOWNSIDE SUPPORTS (From Swing High):
Support Level = ( √High - θ / 180 )²

Where θ (Theta) is the harmonic angle in degrees:
• 90° → Δ = 90 / 180 = 0.500   • 180° → Δ = 180 / 180 = 1.000   • 270° → Δ = 270 / 180 = 1.500   • 360° → Δ = 360 / 180 = 2.000   • 720° → Δ = 720 / 180 = 4.000

Example: If NIFTY makes a low at 23,000:
• √23000 = 151.6575
• 180° Target = (151.6575 + 1.0)² = (152.6575)² = 23,304.3
• 360° Target = (151.6575 + 2.0)² = (153.6575)² = 23,610.6
