100% Leading Trend Predictor
What it is: Tracks the speed (velocity) of Put-Call Ratio changes during the first hour of trading (9:15 AM to 10:15 AM). Option writers leave footprints before spot price makes large moves.
First-Hour PCR Drift Market Meaning Expected Day Type & Action
Bullish Drift (> +0.03 / +3%) Aggressive Put writing (heavy support building) Trend Day Up. Buy CE on any dip to morning VWAP. Hold until 3:00 PM.
Bearish Drift (< -0.03 / -3%) Aggressive Call writing (heavy resistance ceiling) Trend Day Down. Buy PE on any morning pullback. Look for breakdown.
Neutral Drift (-0.03 to +0.03) No institutional writing conviction Range-Bound / Rotational Day. Fade range extremes; scalp only.
